# APA 7 Citation Style Language definition

`apa.csl` is the upstream **APA Style 7th edition** CSL definition, copied without
modification from [citation-style-language/styles](https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles/blob/master/apa.csl),
at upstream commit `3220666a2b6918cccfeca3d86f6f7d9ea9661236` (2026-11-03).

Authors and contributors are recorded in `apa.csl` itself. The style is released
under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported**, as indicated
in its `<rights>` metadata and upstream licensing documentation:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The style is used by the optional, offline `citeproc-py` processor. No online
provider is needed for citation rendering. Other CSL style files may be
installed in this directory in future, with their respective license notices.
